# Skip empty AoE work in stack danger forecasts

The scalar and shared-outcome field forecasts in `CvDangerPlots.cpp` previously scanned all surviving stack members after every accepted threat and called `ChangeValue(id,0)` for an attacker with no positive damage-on-move. The two loops now read the attacker's nonnegative AoE once, after the original direct and collateral updates, and run only when it is positive. Positive damage keeps the original survivor check, candidate order and duplicate multiplicity. No combat, AI or search setting changes.

This preserves numerical wounds and danger values. It can change the private wound container's representation: absent zero entries need not become stored entries, iteration/hash shape can differ, and fewer entries can require less vector capacity. Current selection, interception, collateral and result-extraction consumers read `GetValue(id)`; none consume the evolving ledger's iteration or hash. Shared-outcome admission still accounts retained capacity against the existing limit. Lower capacity may change cache retention and diagnostic counters without changing extracted results. This is not a claim of byte-identical containers or equivalence for a hypothetical future consumer that inspects representation.

`work/test-zero-aoe-danger.py` pins reverted DLL61 baseline `490ee12c41530b7d2675636b5f98f73b2bad09c5`. It requires that the entire danger source differs by only these two exact blocks, compiles complete old/new scalar and outcome bodies with the actual selector/exchange, air, city and collateral helpers, and compares all numerical ID values and selector/strike order. Engine strength and damage services are deterministic test substitutes. It includes missing/explicit zero wounds, negative and positive AoE, player-local ID aliases, duplicate units, sequential casualties, city fire, off-stack interception and the actual shared-outcome capacity/admission code.

The targeted regression passed **4,561,586 checks**. Its random cases eliminated **238,295 zero updates**; a five-unit control removed five survivor reads and five zero writes per threat. These are fixture work counts, not a native speed claim. The existing projected-enemy-damage regression also passed **240,013 checks**.

The older ledger-prototype fixture deliberately refuses this source at its byte-identical attack-sequence assertion. That assertion remains unchanged; it reports the intentional AoE-loop drift before compiling. Its numerical assertions have not been weakened. The new pinned old/new fixture supplies the numerical and exact-change oracle for this patch. Native ordered actions, world censuses and turn timing still require a matched replay.
