# Actual city-combat and capture probes

`work/StackingCityCombatTests.lua` is an inert, work-only helper. Loading it defines `StackCityCombatTests` and prints a loaded marker. It has no event hooks, timers, turns, saves, autoplay, C++ edits or automatic setup. It is registered in CP source as a manual-only VFS import; this preparation does not change the installed game. Use the physical registered-script/include method when root chooses to run it.

Use a disposable game and preserve a pre-fixture normal UI save. The current human must have an active turn and another living major must already own at least one city. The helper picks an empty natural city site accepted by the actual Player:CanFound API, at least4 hexes from existing cities, with no existing units/ownership/improvements within2 hexes and two empty adjacent land plots. The city center must have no feature or resource. It creates a NEW city through Player:Found, then spawns only tagged fixture units. No terrain/river/feature/resource/route/building/technology conversions are used.

The test necessarily changes war, creates a city, and reveals terrain. CleanupUnits removes only this helper's tagged living units; the city, diplomacy and normal game side effects remain. Reload the pre-fixture save for complete restoration. Existing units are snapshotted by owner/ID/type/position/HP/movement and compared after every check. A helper-created city can be reused for the capture test only if its ID/owner still match and no untracked unit entered its city/source/staging tiles. No existing user city is selected or modified. A free unit created by normal founding causes a precondition stop, not deletion of that unit.

## Ranged combat kills the designated garrison

1. `StackCityCombatTests.Try("SetupGarrison")`. This creates two legal city combat occupants: a40-base-strength warrior at1HP, designated as garrison, and a healthy20-strength warrior as replacement. A newly spawned crossbow with30 base ranged strength stands adjacent. The helper verifies the crossbow has no configured collateral targets and garrison absorption is enabled. Only fixture stats are edited.
2. `Try("FireGarrison")`. It records the deterministic real city-damage roll immediately before pushing a normal MISSION_RANGE_ATTACK. The direct garrison-share lower bound must guarantee the1HP garrison dies. No scripted Kill is used to cause the tested casualty.
3. Wait for combat/animations, then `Try("CheckGarrison")`. Required results: exact predicted city-damage increase; unchanged city ownership; original garrison logically dead; healthy replacement takes exactly0 first-shot damage, remains the sole surviving city combat occupant, becomes designated garrison with the correct reverse city link; city strength falls; ranged attacker stays at source unharmed; all original units remain unchanged.
4. Repeat `Try("FireGarrison")`, then wait and `Try("CheckGarrison")`. Only the fixture shooter's moves/attack availability are refreshed for this explicit second shot. The replacement must remain garrison and lose the predicted bounded direct absorption amount; city damage again matches the exact roll and the original garrison stays dead. No turn advancement between stages.

Lua's GetRangeCombatDamage returns city damage R AFTER absorption but does not expose the garrison output. The helper therefore derives the exact integer interval from the DLL equation, rather than treating a rounded inverse as an exact value. Let C be city maxHP, G garrison maxHP, D pre-absorption damage and a=2G. Then R=D-floor(Da/(C+a))=ceil(DC/(C+a)); thus floor((R-1)(C+a)/C)+1 <= D <= floor(R(C+a)/C). Subtract R to obtain the garrison damage interval. It is usually one or two adjacent integers. The first-shot casualty is guaranteed by the lower bound; the second shot checks the actual loss belongs to this tight interval. Non-garrison damage remains exactly0 because this is a non-collateral unit.

A dead entity can persist briefly for combat visualization. Checks use positive HP to distinguish a surviving unit from a pending corpse, while logging exact IDs. Invoke checks only after combat has resolved; repeated checks are harmless, but a failed shot check prevents proceeding to the next shot.

## Real melee capture removes all extra combat occupants

1. `Try("SetupCapture")` reuses the helper-created enemy city where possible, or creates another valid isolated natural city. It places a healthy warrior and healthy archer in the city and reduces only this fixture city's health to1HP. A newly spawned60-strength warrior is the capturer. The city is not the enemy's last city, avoiding an elimination shortcut.
2. `Try("FireCapture")` verifies adjacency and normal move-or-attack legality, records the real seeded city-damage/retaliation calculation, proves the attacker survives and the city loses its last HP, and proves the healthy garrison would survive direct absorption alone. It pushes a normal MISSION_MOVE_TO into the enemy city; no SetXY, Kill, owner assignment or acquire-city call executes the capture.
3. Wait for melee/conquest resolution, then `Try("CheckCapture")`. Keep the city through normal conquest UI rather than razing/liberating it during the check. Required results: city owner changes to the attacker, capturer advances and matches predicted retaliation HP, BOTH former enemy combat occupants are absent/dead, zero old-owner combat survivors remain on the tile, new garrison and reverse link belong to the new owner, and occupancy is legal. Captured-city damage is checked against the actual acquisition cap: min(old city's damage at defeat, floor(new maxHP * CITY_CAPTURE_DAMAGE_PERCENT /100)), capped below new maxHP. The old/new city IDs are logged because acquisition may allocate a new city object. All original units must remain unchanged.

If a civilization/promotion grants additional on-kill/on-capture healing or other post-combat effects, the exact attacker-HP check may expose that confound; preserve logs and inspect it instead of suppressing the mismatch. The default fixture uses basic resource-free melee defenders/capturer and a non-collateral crossbow. SetupGarrison accepts `rangedStrength`; SetupCapture accepts `attackerStrength` if a fixture's city-strength balance makes a stated precondition fail. Such a changed fixture must be recorded.

All stages emit STACKCITY PASS/FAIL, original-unit changes, deterministic rolls, garrison IDs, strength and remaining occupant counts. A successful setup or direct Kill bookkeeping check is not evidence for these real combat requirements.
